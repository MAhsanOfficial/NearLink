```mermaid
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  USER QUERY (Voice/Text)                    │
│  "Plumber chahiye Defence Phase 2 me"       │
└──────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
               ↓
      [🧠 INTENT AGENT]
      Claude 2.0 Flash

               ↓
      [🌍 DISCOVERY AGENT]
      Google Places + PostgreSQL + OSM

               ↓

      Claude 2.0 Flash
      Ranks by: distance, rating, price

               ↓
      [✅ CONFIRMED BOOKING]
```

- **Frontend**: Next.js 16 + Framer Motion
- **Mobile**: Capacitor (iOS + Android)
